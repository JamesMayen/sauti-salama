# 🕊️ Sauti Salama

### Trusted information. Safer communities.

Sauti Salama is a civic information and community safety platform
designed for the South Sudanese context.

The platform helps people:

- 🔎 Verify potentially harmful information
- 🧭 Understand what is known and what remains uncertain
- 🛡️ Report community safety concerns
- 🕊️ Identify emerging community signals
- 📚 Access trusted civic information
- 📱 Access essential information through low-bandwidth channels

## 🎯 Core Principle

> Verify without amplifying.

Sauti Salama is designed to help people distinguish between
verified information, unverified claims, contested information,
and potentially high-risk content.
